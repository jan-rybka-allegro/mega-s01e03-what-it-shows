Problem 1 · shared parser

The shared parser reads dates month first. People at day-first tenants enter dates day first, so ambiguous dates can change month and day. This affects reports from the app and email, and reports from the booking-v2 form. Example: R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July at Harbour Dental (en-GB, booking-v2). Fix the parser according to the tenant's locale and test ambiguous dates across the affected channels.

Problem 2 · booking-v3 hint

The booking-v3 form displays MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants. People follow that hint, but the form reads the entered date in the tenant's day-first order. Example: R-41230 describes 6 January becoming 1 June at Copper Studio (es-ES, booking-v3, form channel). Make the hint and form interpretation agree with the tenant's locale; test the form path separately from the shared parser.

The two problems share symptoms and subject lines. The browser's intake tag is a guess; do not use it or the subject as the cause. Count each report ID once, then use the tenant directory, locale, booking form, and channel to place it.

Triage and follow-up

Keep one engineering ticket per cause. Reports in desk-notes.json described as one-day moves remain in the parser group under the existing tenant-based rule. Recheck their individual evidence if a later investigation establishes another cause.

The subject-only triage rule and decision 0001 are historical records of why the desk merged these reports. A new decision should replace that rule if the desk adopts cause-based merging; do not edit the accepted decision in place.
